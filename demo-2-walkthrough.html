Demo 2 · Key features

Speak the brief. Shape the deck.

This demo combines natural spoken direction with a connected Figma file. Voice mode makes a long, evolving creative brief easy to steer; the Figma connector lets ChatGPT inspect and edit the live presentation.

Voice mode

Talk through goals, corrections, and new ideas without stopping to write a perfect prompt. Each spoken turn becomes the next instruction in a continuous working session.

Figma connector
